Deutsche Fassung
In short

You can have your Finite account and the data associated with it deleted at any time, free of charge and without giving a reason. There are two ways to do it: in the app, or by sending us an email.

This page is public. You do not need to sign in and you do not need to have Finite installed to read it or to request deletion. The email route also works if you have already uninstalled the app or can no longer sign in.
How to request deletion

Option 1 — in the app

Open Finite and go to Settings → Delete account. Your account and your data are deleted as described below.

Option 2 — by email (also works after uninstalling)

Send an email to support.stopdooming@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: Account deletion
  • Your request that your Finite account be deleted
  • Your username in the app, if you have set one

Please send the email from the address you registered with — that is how we identify the account. If you write from a different address, we may have to ask you an additional question before deleting, so that nobody else can delete your account.

There is no web form and no online account portal: deletion requests are handled by email only.

What gets deleted

All personal data we hold about you is deleted. In concrete terms, this covers every data category listed in our Privacy Policy:

Data What it is
Account & profile data Email address, Firebase UID, username, and your sign-in credentials (the account itself is removed from Firebase Authentication, including a Google sign-in link)
Challenge history All your challenges, past and present, together with their daily logs — including your challenge configuration: monitored apps, block lists (domains), limits, schedules and any free text you entered yourself (motivation)
Usage data Daily usage duration of monitored apps, number of (deliberate) opens, streak and progress
Permission status The recorded times at which a required permission (overlay or Accessibility Service) was withdrawn or restored, and the app's periodic sign of life
Circumvention detection The fallback records of monitored apps used while the Accessibility Service was disabled
Technical installation identifiers Push token (FCM) and Firebase Installations ID stored on your user record
Support requests Email address, username, Firebase UID, subject, message text, device model, Android version and app version from any support request you sent us
Data on your device The app's local database is cleared when you delete the account in the app; it is removed in full when you uninstall Finite
What is retained, and why

One category is kept after deletion:

  • Records of consent — evidence of your acceptance of the Terms and Conditions, the Privacy Policy and the age confirmation (18+). These are retained as proof that consent was given, on the basis of Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (legal obligation).
In this version of Finite, no payment, credit card or bank details are collected or processed. There are therefore no financial or accounting records that would have to be retained for statutory reasons.
How long it takes

We respond to requests within 30 days in accordance with Art. 12(3) GDPR. All personal data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion, with the exception of the records of consent named above.

Deletion is permanent: once your data has been deleted it cannot be restored, and your challenge history and progress are gone for good.
